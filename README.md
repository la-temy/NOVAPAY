# NovaPay – Internal Audit & Policy Framework Build

## Project Overview
This project develops two information security policies for NovaPay, a fictional FinTech company operating a payment gateway and customer-facing banking application.

The objective is to strengthen the protection of customer and payment information across employee devices, corporate systems, Azure-hosted services and third-party integrations.

## Policies Developed
- **BYOD Policy:** Defines security requirements for personal devices accessing company resources, including encryption, authentication, patching, remote wipe and lost-device reporting.
- **Access Control Policy:** Establishes least privilege, role-based access, segregation of duties, MFA, privileged access management, periodic access reviews and timely access removal.

## Key Findings – Policy Gap Analysis

| Identified Gap | Recommended Improvement |
|---|---|
| BYOD compliance is not verified before devices connect. | Require device security checks and approval, supported by MDM. |
| No formal register of approved personal devices. | Maintain a device register containing user, device, approval date and access status. |
| Leavers' access removal is not time-bound. | Require immediate notification and access revocation within 24 hours of departure. |

**ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A alignment:**
- A.8.1 – User endpoint devices
- A.5.9 – Inventory of information and other associated assets
- A.5.18 – Access rights

## Internal Audit Questions
Five audit questions were developed to assess:
1. BYOD device security compliance.
2. Timely reporting of lost or stolen devices.
3. Authorisation of user access.
4. Completion of periodic access reviews.
5. Timely access modification and removal.

Evidence includes device compliance records, incident reports, access requests, approvals, review records and account logs.
